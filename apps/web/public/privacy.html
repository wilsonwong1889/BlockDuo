Privacy Policy

BLOKDUO · Last updated 14 August 2026

BLOKDUO is a block puzzle game at blokduo.ca. This page describes what the game stores, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

You do not create an account and you are never asked for your name, email address, or payment details. The game stores your progress — score, coins, gems, level — against a random identifier generated on your device. Advertising, where shown, is served by Google and may use cookies.

What is stored, and where

Data Where it lives Why
A random player ID and access token Your browser, and our server To recognise your progress on your next visit without a login
Display name you choose Our server Shown on leaderboards and to duo partners
Progress: scores, coins, gems, level, streaks, statistics Our server The game's progression and leaderboards
Friends you add Our server The friends leaderboard
Board state, settings, sound preferences Your browser only So a reload resumes your game
IP address Held in memory briefly, never written to storage Rate limiting, so one source cannot flood sign-ups or rooms
Crash reports Written to our server logs, not to a database To find out what broke when something breaks

The display name is the only field you type, so please do not put personal information in it — it is shown publicly on leaderboards.

Advertising

BLOKDUO may show advertising supplied by Google, including Google AdSense. Where it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • More detail is in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising, if offered inside the game in exchange for a reward, is always optional. You never lose progress you already earned by declining one.

Who else sees your data

Nobody buys it, because it is not for sale. The game runs on Cloudflare, which processes traffic on our behalf, and advertising is served by Google as described above. Leaderboard entries — a display name and a score — are public by design.

Children

BLOKDUO is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information — realistically, a display name — contact us and it will be removed.

Erasing your data

Clearing your browser's site data for blokduo.ca removes the local copy and the identifier that links you to your progress on the server. To have the server-side record deleted outright, contact us with your player code, which is on your profile screen.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), the UK/EU GDPR, or similar — you may have the right to access, correct, or delete what is held about you, and to complain to a regulator. Ask and we will act on it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the game.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@blokduo.ca, or see the contact page.
