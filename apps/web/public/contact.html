Contact

BLOKDUO is run by one person. Mail goes to a real inbox and gets read.

Email

hello@blokduo.ca — anything at all: a bug, an idea, a question about how something works.

privacy@blokduo.ca — data requests specifically: seeing what is held about you, or having it deleted. See the privacy policy for what that covers.

Things worth including

Not required, but they turn a slow reply into a quick one.

  • For a bug: what you were doing, what happened, and what you expected instead. Whether it was Classic or Duo. The browser and device help.
  • For a lost account: your player code, which is on your profile screen and looks like BD-XXXXXXXX. Without it there is no way to tell which progress is yours.
  • For a data request: the same player code, since that is the only handle on your record.
Changed address and lost your progress? You may not need to write at all. The game used to live at a different address, and progress is saved per address. Open the old one and use the “Move my progress” button on the home screen — it carries your coins, gems and stats across in one click.

What this is not

There is no support team, no ticket queue and no promised response time. It is one person who will answer when they see it. Please do not send anything urgent or anything that matters legally to this address without following up.
