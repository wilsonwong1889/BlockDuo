How to play BLOKDUO

The whole game in a minute, then the details that decide whether you are good at it.

The basic loop

  • Drag a piece from your tray onto the 8×8 board. Pieces cannot be rotated for free.
  • Fill a whole row or column and it clears.
  • Nothing falls after a clear. This is not Tetris — the holes you leave are permanent until you fill them yourself.
  • You hold three pieces at a time. A new set of three arrives once all three are used.
  • The game ends when none of the pieces you are holding fit anywhere on the board.

A new game deals an opening hand chosen to make early line-building friendlier, so the first minute is not decided by luck.

Scoring

Points come from three places, and the third one is where good players pull ahead.

Source Points
Each cell of a placed piece 1
Each line cleared 10 base
Clearing 2 lines at once +20 bonus
Clearing 3 at once +60 bonus
Clearing 4 at once +120 bonus
Clearing 5 at once +200 bonus
Wiping the board completely +300

Notice the shape of that table. Four separate single clears are worth far less than one placement that clears four lines. Setting up a big simultaneous clear is almost always better than taking each line as it becomes available — that asymmetry is the whole game.

Streak multipliers

Clear on consecutive turns and a multiplier builds: +0.5× for each consecutive clearing move, up to a ceiling of 4×. Place a piece without clearing anything and the streak resets to nothing.

This is the real skill in the game. A player who clears every other turn scores a fraction of a player who clears four turns running, even with identical placements. Sometimes the right move is a worse placement that keeps the streak alive.

Powers

Powers cost gems and are limited by what you can pay, with one exception. They are part of the game record, not a client-side convenience, so a game played with powers still ranks.

Power Cost What it does
Undo 1 gem Take the last placed piece back off the board. Three per game, maximum.
Rotate 2 gems Turn one held piece a quarter turn clockwise.
Reroll 3 gems Throw the whole hand away and deal another three.

Coins, gems and the wheel

A finished game's score becomes coins one-for-one, with a survival bonus: +0.25× for every 25 pieces you survived, up to a maximum of 2×. A long game is worth more than a short one with the same score.

Gems buy powers and are won from the wheel. Every player gets one free spin a day. Beyond that a spin costs 10,000 coins, or you can watch an advert for up to three extra spins a day. The wheel pays 1, 2, 3, 10 or 50 gems — 50 lands on one spin in a hundred.

Adverts are never required. Declining one never removes progress you already earned, and no advert interrupts a game in progress.

Ranked and Casual

Casual Classic is played for coins and your own best score. Ranked Classic has no powers — no undo, no rotate, no reroll — and it is the mode the leaderboard is made of. Your best completed Ranked game stands until the board resets on Monday, UTC.

Duo: two players, one board

Duo is the mode that makes BLOKDUO different. Two people play the same 8×8 board at the same time, sharing one tray of three pieces, taking alternating turns.

  • One player creates a room and sends the link. Following it drops the other player straight in — no account, no friend request.
  • Classic Duo gives each player 60 seconds a turn. Ranked Duo gives 5 seconds, and only Ranked results reach the leaderboards.
  • Lose your connection and your seat is held for a minute, so a dropped signal does not end the game.
  • The score is the team's, not one player's.

The tray is the thing to think about. You are not just finding a good spot for your piece — you are deciding what your partner is left holding. The best Duo players play slightly worse individual moves to leave a better board behind.

Playing offline

Once the game has been opened once, Classic mode works with no connection at all. Duo needs the network, for obvious reasons.
