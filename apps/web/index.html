BLOKDUO

Fill rows and columns. Solo, or two-up on one board.

BLOKDUO is a free 8×8 block puzzle. You are dealt three pieces at a time and drop them onto a grid; fill a whole row or column and it clears. Nothing falls afterwards, so the gaps you leave are yours to deal with. In Duo mode two people share one board and one tray of pieces, taking alternating turns, live.

It runs in the browser. No download, no account, and nothing to install.
