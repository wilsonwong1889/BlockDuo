Your account

You do not create an account. Progress is stored against a random identifier generated on your device, described in the privacy policy. Two consequences worth understanding:

  • Clearing your browser's site data removes the identifier, and with it your route back to your progress. There is no password to recover it with.
  • Anyone with access to your browser, or to a transfer link you generated, has access to your progress. Treat a transfer link like a password.

Fair play

Please do not:

  • Submit game records you did not play, or modify the game client to produce scores you did not earn. Games are replayed and verified on the server, so this mostly wastes your time, but accounts doing it may be removed from the leaderboards.

  • Choose a display name that is abusive, impersonates somebody, or that you would not want shown publicly. Names appear on leaderboards and to other players.
  • Interfere with other players' games, or with the service itself.

Access may be withdrawn for any of the above, without notice. Given there are no purchases, nothing is refundable.
